# Study notebook

Direction selected by the user: warm paper, ink navy, one amber accent, readable local fonts, light theme, and quiet transitions. PocketReview is the working project name.

Reading this as a mobile study utility for Filipino students in a study-notebook visual language: ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 1 / MOTION 1.

- Warm paper (#faf6ed) connects the interface to printed lessons without decorative textures.
- Ink navy (#172b4d) keeps long source passages readable and gives headings a consistent hierarchy.
- Amber (#ad5a00) marks the current action and notebook rule, not every component.
- System fonts keep text legible without a font download or offline dependency.
- Narrow reading measures, generous line height, and source-like quotation blocks serve careful inspection.
- Mobile uses stacked content, intermediate widths expand the reading area, and wide screens place setup beside evidence.
- Fixed light theme follows the selected printed-notebook direction. No theme toggle is in scope.
- Motion is limited to hover/active feedback and respects reduced motion.
- The simple notebook icon is a working-name placeholder for installation, not a final brand logo.
# Setup delivery rationale

- Georgia gives source text and answers the familiar feel of a study notebook; system sans-serif separates controls and evidence labels.
- The two-column desktop spread places evidence beside setup, while mobile stacks it beneath the primary task for readable text and full-width controls.
- Solid ruled separators organize actual checks and results without decorative cards, shadows, or background textures.
- Amber marks the model preparation action; navy text and focus outlines preserve contrast against warm paper.
- The fixed light theme follows the approved study-notebook direction. Motion is limited to native loading progress and focus/hover feedback.
